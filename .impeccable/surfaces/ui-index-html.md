# WRECKAGE plugin UI

Scope: the whole plugin window (ui/index.html in a JUCE WebView, fixed 1200x750 stage scaled to the window). Mode: Operate (play, tweak, save presets), with Experience moments (tube, rust, birthday).

Audience/job: Jacob (WRECKEM), FL Studio on Windows, dim room. Auditions presets, tweaks, saves and tags his own like he does in Serum.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A condemned concrete wall you play. Refuses the category default of a flat dark synth panel with a skin; everything is cut into or bolted onto board-formed concrete.

OWN-WORLD: Raw grey board-formed concrete slabs split by black shadow gaps, matte black cast knobs, engraved (recessed, textured) condensed caps lettering, one accent: rust coral #E0745A for value arcs, fader fills, the tube and the scope. Rust drips and anchor-hole weeps grow with the RUST macro. WRECKEM spray tag on the top-right slab.

STORY: He opens it, the tube flickers with the sound, he grabs RUST and the wall corrodes. CRATES slides out a shelf of tagged presets; SAVE AS names, tags and signs his own.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top band: WRECKAGE engraved left, preset plate with arrows and CRATES centred over a caged fluorescent tube (output meter, flashes on loud/fast hits), WRECKEM tag right. Row of seven slabs: OSC A, OSC B, SUB/NOISE, FILTER (largest CUTOFF), AMP ENV, FILTER ENV, LFO. Bottom: bench with scope screen and SCOPE/DIRT/SPACE/VOICE tabs; WRECK slab with FOLD/TAPE/FUZZ/CRUSH selector, DRIVE and the giant RUST knob.

FORM: Condemned Panel (Impeccable's pick, user-chosen), comp-4 approved after two rounds (colder, flatter, Owens concrete with rust coral). Seed key b66ca291. Signature interaction: the tube meter reacting and flashing; RUST corroding the wall.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Approved comp: .impeccable/mocks/comp-4.png. User changes after approval: tube reacts to sound and flashes when loud/fast; lettering engraved into the concrete (not floating light ink). User relaxed the plate pixel gate ("Relax it, keep going").
