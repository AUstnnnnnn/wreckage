WRECKAGE
for WRECKEM. Oct 29 2026. From Austin.

WINDOWS / FL STUDIO
1. Copy WRECKAGE.vst3 into  C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\
2. FL Studio: Options > Manage plugins > Find more plugins. WRECKAGE shows up under Generators.
3. WRECKAGE.exe runs on its own without FL if you want to just play it.

MAC
WRECKAGE.vst3 goes in ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/, WRECKAGE.component in ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/.
It isn't signed, so run this once in Terminal:
  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/WRECKAGE.vst3 ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/WRECKAGE.component

YOUR PRESETS
Saved as plain .wreck files in Documents\WRECKAGE\Presets. Back them up, send them to people,
drop other people's in there. CRATES opens the browser: SAVE AS to name, tag and sign a patch,
click the tags to filter, star the ones you keep coming back to. Arrow keys step through the list.

PLAYING
Computer keys A to L play notes, Z and X change octave. Double-click any knob to reset it,
shift-drag for fine moves. The mod wheel pushes RUST.

There's more in here. Try the wall.
